Jabil Digital Transformation

InControl & Factory of the Future

A connected view of transformation sequencing, governance, and executive performance to turn strategy into measurable impact.

About the Project

This project examines Jabil's InControl and Factory of the Future transformation through three connected management lenses: sequencing, governance, and executive measurement. The resulting artifacts show how foundational capabilities enable deployment, how decision rights support scale, and how leadership can monitor transformation health.

Target Audience

Executive leaders and transformation stakeholders responsible for funding, governing, scaling, and evaluating enterprise digital initiatives.

Key Benefits

Creates a connected view of transformation sequencing, decision ownership, execution health, and business outcomes so leadership can identify dependencies and act on issues quickly.

What's Novel

Rather than treating the roadmap, governance model, and QBR as separate artifacts, our approach connects them into a single decision-support framework. Initiatives flow from dependency-aware sequencing into explicit decision rights and measurable executive oversight.
